| 2026-08-07 | [Group threads got their members' faces](2026-08-07-group-avatar-stack.md) | change | A group row drew one avatar from the thread's name, so every group looked like a person. `AvatarStack` draws the members instead. The handoff was revised mid-work and **changed approach, not details** — from a hand-rolled scatter (absolute tiles alternating top/bottom) to MUI's `AvatarGroup` with a tight negative overlap and a `tight`/`row` variant; both were built, the first discarded. `slotProps.additionalAvatar` **does not exist in MUI v9** and is a type error — the second time in a day the handoff was a major behind on slot names, after `Checkbox`'s `inputProps`, which v9 drops *silently*. Tests caught two things reading did not: `AvatarGroup` gives up a face slot to the surplus once the total overflows `max`, so six members draw two faces and "+4" (the label had claimed "3 more"), and `fallbackName` wins over a lone member's name. Colour comes from the member id, diverging from the handoff's name-length rule, which would give one person different colours in different places. Verified by 69 tests with geometry asserted on computed pixels; **not rendered in a browser**, and every face is initials until #47 stops `toThread` discarding member avatars. |

| 2026-08-07 | [Typing and presence stopped going to everyone, and started saying who](2026-08-07-hub-scoped-typing.md) | change | #37 fixed message fan-out but left the hub on the 1:1 assumptions. Now addressed to the thread's participants minus the typist, and refused for a non-participant, answering identically for "no such thread" so it is not an oracle. Presence narrowed to peers. Both halves proved by breaking them (reverting to `Clients.All` fails 3; removing the check fails the non-participant test). 0 warnings on a `--no-incremental` build — an earlier 0-warning claim was from an incremental build hiding a CS8603. Addressing only: nothing here proves a byte reaches a browser. |

Verified at API and adapter level, 0 warnings, 52 .NET + 64 client tests; **not re-checked in a browser**. |

Landed as errors, not a warning ratchet: 20 findings, all resolved in one sitting. Two things the research did not predict: **Prettier's reflow silently broke an `oxlint-disable-next-line`** by expanding a one-liner into a block and moving the offending statement away from the directive, and **Prettier needed two passes** on a method chain in `smoke.test.tsx` before `--check` agreed with `--write`. Verified: `npm run verify` green, 61/61 tests, vite build succeeds, and the react-hooks bridge proved live by linting a deliberate violation rather than trusting an empty output. | 2026-08-05 | [Custom domain and email deliverability (issue #30)](2026-08-05-custom-domain-and-email-deliverability.md) | change | Activation email had landed in spam since launch not because of the template or provider but because a `gmail.com` From address relayed through Brevo can never pass SPF/DKIM alignment — only Google can authenticate as gmail.com. Fixed by buying `vtechsolutions.site` and serving the app at `chat.vtechsolutions.site`, DNS kept at the registrar (no `zone:` in `.do/app.yaml`, so DO waits on an external CNAME rather than taking over the zone) rather than moved to DigitalOcean. Two settings fail silently if missed: `Cors__AllowedOrigins__1` (or SignalR just won't connect, no CORS error) and `App__PublicUrl` set explicitly rather than `${APP_URL}` (or every email link keeps pointing at the old host). Verified live: all 8 DNS records resolve as intended (and the Brevo ownership TXT is actually at the apex, not a `brevo-code` subdomain despite the name), cert valid to 2026-11-03, http→https redirect and `/health` both correct. |
